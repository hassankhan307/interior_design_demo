    Calacatta & Roman Travertine

    Monolithic marble kitchen islands and honed porous travertine wall panels with subtle natural veining.

    Origin: European FSC Certified

    Fluted European White Oak

    Custom architectural slatting, concealed doors, and hand-rubbed organic oil finishes that age gracefully.

    Finish: Hand-Brushed Satin

    Brushed Champagne Brass

    Custom sanitary fittings, cabinet hardware, and recessed shadowline architectural trim.

    Origin: Belgium & Italy

    Bouclé & Raw Textured Linen

    Heavyweight natural drapes and curved sculptural seating that invite tactile comfort and acoustic quietude.

    Texture: Warm Matte

    Continuous floor and wall transitions without grout lines, creating monolithic spa-like wet rooms and modern floors.
